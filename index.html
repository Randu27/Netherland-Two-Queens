🌴 DISCOVER THE HIGHLIGHTS OF SRI LANKA

Discover the highlights of Sri Lanka with an unforgettable journey through Nuwara Eliya, Ella, Yala and Mirissa. Explore the cool mountain landscapes and tea plantations of Nuwara Eliya, including the breathtaking Horton Plains. Continue to Ella for a scenic train ride, a visit to the iconic Nine Arch Bridge.

Experience the wild side of Sri Lanka with an exciting Yala National Park Safari, before travelling to the beautiful southern coast of Mirissa. Explore the peaceful Koggala Lagoon, visit a Sea Turtle Hatchery, and enjoy the tropical beaches and coastal charm of southern Sri Lanka.

This journey combines nature, adventure, wildlife, culture and relaxation into one memorable Sri Lankan experience. 🇱🇰🌴✨
